Cruinne is a planet. A full, round, three-mooned planet occupying a fixed point in the Silver Deep. The other planes exist as coincident worlds — each sharing the same position in space, each expressing it differently. The mountains of the Feywild and the mountains of Cruinne are, broadly speaking, the same mountains.

The gods protect the Wheel. They answer prayers, hold the line against incursion, and have been here as long as anyone can remember. Whether that means they have always been here is a different question. One that certain people have found very expensive to ask aloud.

— Wheel & Wrack Touring Society, Third Edition
